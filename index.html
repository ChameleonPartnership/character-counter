What is this character counter for?

Character Counter Pro counts characters, words, lines, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and platform limits in real time. It is built for writers, marketers, SEO teams, developers, students, support teams, and product managers who need instant text-length feedback without sending draft text to a server.

The tool is useful when a piece of writing must fit a hard limit, such as a social media post, app-store field, form validation message, email subject line, title tag, or meta description. It also helps with soft limits where length affects readability: onboarding copy, help-centre answers, product descriptions, abstracts, short bios, and push notifications.

How the character counter works

The counter runs in your browser. When you type or paste text into the box, the page reads the current value of the text area and calculates the visible statistics immediately. Characters with spaces use the full string length. Characters without spaces remove whitespace before counting. Words are counted from word-like sequences, lines come from line breaks, sentences are estimated from sentence-ending punctuation, and paragraphs are counted from blocks of text separated by blank lines.

Because the calculation happens locally, the counter is fast enough for long drafts and private enough for everyday editing. You can paste a newsletter paragraph, a draft tweet, a product summary, or a long article excerpt and see the numbers change while you revise. The browser may store interface preferences, such as theme and whether the detailed panel is open, but the typed text itself is not saved by this site.

The platform limit bars are simple writing aids rather than official publishing checks. They show commonly used limits and recommended ranges for channels where length matters. A post can still be unsuitable because of links, media, language, or platform-specific rules, so use the counter as an editing checkpoint before you paste the final text into the destination app.

Step-by-step guide with examples

  1. Paste your draft into the text box. Example input: "Launch offer ends Friday. Save 20% on annual plans before midnight." The counter reports 67 characters with spaces, 58 without spaces, 11 words, and one sentence.
  2. Check the primary limit for your channel. If that same sentence is an email subject line, it is longer than the 50-character guide, so tighten it to: "Save 20% on annual plans by Friday." That version is 36 characters and keeps the main benefit.
  3. Use the word count and reading time for longer copy. Example input: a 750-word landing page section reads as about three minutes at 250 words per minute. If the section is meant to be a quick product overview, that may be too long; if it is a support article, the length may be acceptable.
  4. Open the detailed statistics panel when the exact composition matters. For example, a form rule might require at least one number and one special character. The detailed panel separates numbers, spaces, and punctuation so you can verify the copy quickly.
  5. Copy the final statistics when you need to document an edit. The "Copy Stats" button creates a short report that can be pasted into an editorial checklist, SEO ticket, QA note, or content handoff.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I count characters in text?

Simply paste or type your text into the input box at the top. Character counts update in real-time, showing both characters with and without spaces. Our tool instantly counts all characters, including letters, numbers, spaces, and symbols.

What's the difference between characters and words?

Characters include every letter, number, space, and symbol. Words are counted as sequences separated by spaces. For example, 'Hello world' is 11 characters (including the space) but only 2 words.

What are platform character limits used for?

Different platforms enforce different character limits optimised for their format and user experience. Twitter allows 280 per post, LinkedIn allows 3,000 for deeper storytelling, email subject lines are optimal at 50 characters, and SEO page titles should stay under 60 characters for full display in search results.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated by dividing your text's total word count by the selected words-per-minute (WPM) rate. The default is 250 WPM (average reader), but you can adjust to 200 WPM (slow readers) or 300 WPM (fast readers) depending on text complexity and audience.

Is Character Counter Pro free to use?

Yes, the character counter is free to use. No account sign-up, credit card, or paid plan is required, and the core counting workflow remains available directly in the browser.

What's the best character count to aim for on Twitter?

Twitter's limit is 280 characters. For maximum engagement and easy retweets, aim for 100–260 characters — long enough to convey your message completely, but short enough to allow space for replies and comments from followers.

How do I optimise my LinkedIn post length?

LinkedIn posts can be up to 3,000 characters. For optimal engagement, aim for 1,500–2,500 characters — long enough to tell a compelling story with personality and details, but short enough to keep readers engaged without requiring them to click 'see more'.

Why do email subject lines have a 50-character limit?

Email clients display approximately 40–50 characters on mobile devices and 60 on desktop. Subject lines longer than 50 characters are truncated on mobile, which reduces visibility and can lower click-through rates. Keep them at 30–50 characters for optimal display across devices.

What's the ideal length for an SEO meta description?

Google displays 150–160 characters on desktop and 120 characters on mobile. Write meta descriptions between 150–160 characters to ensure they display fully in search results, and include your primary keyword naturally within that space.

Can I count special characters separately?

Yes. Click 'Show Details' under your text to see a complete breakdown including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, spaces, and special characters counted separately. This is useful for password requirements and form validation rules.

What counts as a 'word' in your counter?

A word is any sequence of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Numbers and hyphenated words (e.g., 'well-structured') each count as single words. Standalone punctuation marks do not count as words.

Can I use this tool on my phone?

Yes, Character Counter Pro is fully responsive and works on all devices including smartphones and tablets. Simply visit the site on your mobile browser and start typing — all features work exactly the same on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

Does the counter save my text?

No. The text you enter is processed in the browser for the current session and is not uploaded to a Character Counter server. If you refresh or close the page, the typed text is not preserved by this site.

Why do counts differ between tools?

Different tools define words, punctuation, emoji, and line breaks differently. This counter is intended for practical editing decisions. For a strict platform or database field, always test the final copy in that system as well.
