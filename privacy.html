Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Character Counter is designed as a browser-based text counting tool maintained by Chameleon Partnership. This Privacy Policy explains what happens when you use the site, including browser storage, advertising cookies, and third-party advertising disclosures.

Text Processing

Your text stays in your browser

Text counting and analysis happens client-side in your web browser. The text you type or paste into Character Counter is not sent to a Character Counter server for counting, storage, or review.

No account is required

Character Counter does not require registration, and we do not ask you to provide your name, email address, or other account details to use the tool.

Browser Storage

localStorage

Character Counter may use your browser's localStorage to remember interface preferences, including the theme setting, the detailsVisible detailed statistics preference, and the characterCounterCookieConsent banner choice. This information is stored on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Google AdSense

Advertising cookies

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure ad performance, and help detect fraud or abuse.

Personalised ads

Ads may be personalised based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. You can review or change personalised advertising choices in Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about broader opt-out choices at aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
