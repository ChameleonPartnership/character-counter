How Character Counter Pro handles text and browser storage

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Character Counter Pro is a static, browser-based text tools hub maintained by Chameleon Partnership. This policy explains local text processing, browser storage, consent and contact details.

Text processing

Text counting, conversion, cleanup and generation happen client-side in your browser. The text you type or paste into the tools is not sent to a Character Counter Pro server for counting, storage, conversion or review.

No account is required. We do not ask for your name, email address or payment details to use the tools.

Browser storage and consent

The site may use localStorage to remember interface preferences, including theme choice, details visibility and the cookie consent banner choice. This data is stored on your device and can be cleared through your browser settings.

The consent banner is included on every page. It explains essential browser storage and the possibility of future advertising cookies if ads are enabled after review.

Advertising status

This build does not include ad units or ad loader scripts. If advertising is enabled later, this policy will be updated to explain the advertising partner, cookie use and opt-out choices before ad code is added.

Owner and contact

Character Counter Pro is maintained by Chameleon Partnership as a free browser-based utility hub for text work.

For privacy or site-owner questions, email paulodonnell@chameleonpartnership.com.
