About Character Counter Pro

A practical browser-based text measurement tool

Who maintains the site?

Character Counter Pro is maintained by Chameleon Partnership. The site exists to make everyday text-length checks quick, transparent, and easy to complete without an account.

The tool is designed for writers, editors, marketers, developers, students, and support teams who need to measure copy before publishing it in constrained fields such as social media posts, email subjects, SEO metadata, forms, product descriptions, and help desk replies.

How the tool is built

The counter runs in the browser with lightweight HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Text entered into the tool is processed locally for character count, word count, line count, sentence count, paragraph count, reading time, and platform-limit progress bars.

Character Counter Pro aims to keep the main workflow simple: paste text, review the numbers, revise the copy, and copy the statistics when needed. Supporting guides on the blog explain common limits and editing decisions in more detail.
