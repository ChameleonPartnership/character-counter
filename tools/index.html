All text tools

Every tool on this site runs in your browser. Use the hub to move from counting into cleanup, formatting and draft preparation without sending text to a server.

Word counter

Count words, sentences, paragraphs, reading time, speaking time and keyword density.

Case converter

Convert text into UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, alternating case and inverse case.

Remove line breaks

Strip line breaks, collapse whitespace and trim pasted text from PDFs, emails and documents.

Remove duplicate lines

Deduplicate lists with optional trim and ignore-case controls.

Slug generator

Create clean URL slugs with separator and stop-word options.

Lorem ipsum generator

Generate placeholder paragraphs, sentences or words for layouts and content models.

Reading time calculator

Estimate reading and speaking time across articles, scripts, help copy and presentations.

How the hub fits together

Start with the character counter when the problem is length. Move to the word counter when you need density, timing or structure. Use the cleanup tools when copy arrives from a PDF, spreadsheet or email thread with broken line breaks or duplicated rows. Use the case converter and slug generator when the words are approved but the format needs to match code, URLs or content systems.
