Character Counter Blog

Every platform you write for has a hidden constraint: Twitter's post limit, the 160 characters of an SMS, the meta description that Google truncates at around 155, the subject line that gets cut off in an inbox. Knowing the exact limit is only half the job. The other half is knowing how to write well within it.

This blog covers the practical side of length limits: the character budgets of the major social platforms, the discipline of concise writing, and the precise counts that matter for search engine optimisation. Each guide is built around numbers you can verify and techniques you can use the same day.

The limits matter because they shape where your message gets cut off. A tweet that runs to 300 characters is truncated, a meta description longer than about 155 characters is ellipsized in search results, and an SMS over 160 characters splits into two messages. Writing well within a limit is not about squeezing text; it is about deciding what the reader must see first. The guides below cover the exact numbers and the techniques for making them work for you.
