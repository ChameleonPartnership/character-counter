Use this page as a quick reference before approving copy. Some entries are hard platform limits, while others are practical display targets used by editors to avoid truncation. Always test the final version inside the destination platform before publishing.

How to use these numbers

Start by identifying whether the number is a rejection limit, a billing boundary or a display target. SMS, app names and many product fields are strict. Email subjects and metadata are usually about what the reader sees first. Social posts often allow long copy while hiding it behind a preview.

Paste the final draft into the character counter, record the count, then test the same copy in the platform composer. This two-step check catches both editorial problems and platform-specific rules.
